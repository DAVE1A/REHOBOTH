ABOUT US

Our Story

Rehoboth ENT Specialist Hospital was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide exceptional ear, nose, and throat care to the people of Osun State and beyond. What began as a modest clinic has grown into a premier specialty hospital recognized for clinical excellence and compassionate care.

Our founder, Dr. Adedeji Taiwo, established the hospital after recognizing the need for specialized ENT services in the region. With a team of dedicated professionals, we've been transforming lives through innovative treatments and personalized care for over a decade.

The name "Rehoboth" comes from the biblical reference meaning "room enough," reflecting our commitment to making space for all patients to receive the care they need in a welcoming environment.
