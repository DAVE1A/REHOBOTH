AUDIOLOGY

We offer Audiological Assessment and Evaluation (Hearing Tests) solution and treatment for Children and Adults

such as :

  • Pure Tone Audiology (PTA)
  • Tympanometry
  • Oto-Acoustic Emission (OAE)
  • Transient Otoacoustic Emission (TOAE)
  • Distortion Otoacoustic Emission (DPOE)
  • Auditory Brainstem Response (ABR)
  • Hearing Aid Dispensing
  • Vestibular Assessment

Safety is one of our core values at Rehoboth E.N.T Hospital.
